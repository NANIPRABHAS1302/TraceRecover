Five-Layer TraceRecover Reliability Pipeline

Every tool-augmented query flows through five auditable stages separating trusted committed evidence from failed or discarded steps.

1 Plan

Select allowlisted tools and multi-hop execution plan.

2 Execute

Validate arguments, invoke tools, and log step provenance.

3 Recover

Detect and localize faults; heal via Retry, Repair, Switch, or Replan.

4 Verify

Extract atomic claims and ground strictly against committed evidence.

5 Decide

Estimate trace uncertainty and emit ANSWER, WARN, or ABSTAIN.
